Join the club

Every player registers on PlayHQ for the 2026/27 season. Seniors are open now; junior grades are full with a waitlist.

RegisterFeesPayment & refundsJunior waitlist

Register in three steps

PlayHQ handles both your Parnell membership and Auckland Cricket's scoring platform. ClubHub is no longer used.

  1. Create a PlayHQ account

    One account per family. Add each player you're registering.

  2. Choose Parnell Cricket Club

    Pick your grade by school year (juniors and youth) or team (seniors).

  3. Pay in full

    Subscriptions are paid in full at registration. Your team manager is in touch before the first game.
