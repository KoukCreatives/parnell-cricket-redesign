Our story

168 years in the making

New Zealand's oldest cricket club – from a team of teenagers on Albert Barracks to Auckland's Club of the Year.

Keep scrolling →
1858

The Juveniles beat Auckland

On 17 March at Albert Barracks, a Parnell side of teenagers won by an innings and 28 runs – bowling Auckland out for 11.

1860

Five in the first Auckland side

Auckland challenged and beat Wellington. Five Parnell members were in that very first Auckland team.

1864

New Zealand's first first-class match

Otago v Canterbury. Parnell's John Kissling umpired and GK Turton played.

1884

Joining Auckland Cricket

Parnell affiliates with the Auckland Cricket Association, formed the year before, and plays on the Domain.

1898–1914

Five championships

Under the new District Scheme, Parnell wins the Auckland championship five times.

1940

Bert Sutcliffe arrives

Straight from Takapuna Grammar – possibly our greatest batsman, and a Wisden Cricketer of the Year in 1950.

2001

A home at Shore Road

Invited to the newly reclaimed Shore Road Reserve: nets, a clubroom, then the Terry Jarvis Centre.

2024& 25

Club of the Year

Auckland Cricket's Simmonds & Osborne Trophy – back to back.

    Contact us
    How do I register?

    All players register on PlayHQ for the 2026/27 season – this covers both club membership and Auckland Cricket's scoring platform. Subscriptions are paid in full at registration.

    Junior grades are full – what now?

    Email Zak or Steve to join the waitlist. Places do come up during the season.

    Which grade will I play in?

    Juniors and youth are placed by school year (as at 2026). Seniors are graded by ability, from Premier through to social and T20 teams.

    Where are games played?

    Our home is Shore Road, with indoor training at the Terry Jarvis Centre. Away games are listed in the draw on game day.

    What if it rains?

    Check draws & cancellations on the morning of play.

    Can I get a refund?

    Full refund (less a NZ$30 admin fee) until the first day of Term 4; 50% until the first day of play; no refund once the season starts.
