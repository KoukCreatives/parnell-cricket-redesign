Junior & Youth Winter Academies

Registrations for the 2026 winter academies are open. Focused on age and stage, with programmes tailored for junior boys/mixed, youth boys, junior girls and youth girls. The focus is skills development through a pathway that prepares players for junior club cricket, college cricket and ultimately senior cricket – building a lifelong love of the game.

Academies are popular and numbers are limited – most fill up, so enrol early.

Boys & mixed academies

Seven to eight skill sessions led by Ian West with our coaching staff, finishing with pre-season matches – and the Future Peaches joining some Premier squad trainings and games. Register in the grade your child will play in 2026/27.

  • Under 10 – NZ$250: for players from Year 4 in 2025/26 – the basics of hardball cricket, like wearing gear properly and seam position.
  • Under 11 & 12 – NZ$290: Auckland Cricket's age-and-stage rules and adapting your game for JTS cricket.
  • Under 13 – NZ$290: preparing for colts and college cricket – the change in format and in your physique.
  • Years 9 & 10 – NZ$290: modelled on the Future Peaches – colts and 1st/2nd XI college cricket, plus an introduction to Parnell's senior programme. Some players are invited to train with the Future Peaches (NZ$320).
  • Years 11–13, Future Peaches – NZ$320: designed by Ian West five years ago to link our oldest youth players to the senior club, whatever their college selection.

Girls academies

Open to Parnell and non-Parnell players, on Sunday afternoons and evenings through Terms 2 and 3. Players are grouped by age and skill once registrations close (3 May 2026); fees are paid term by term.

  • Term 2: Sundays 10, 17, 24 May and 7, 14, 21 June (no sessions King's Birthday or Matariki weekends)
  • Term 3: Sundays 2, 9, 16, 23, 30 August and 6 September
  • Junior Girls Years 3–6 softball: 60-minute sessions for U9 & U11 softball players, in small ability groups.
  • Junior Girls Years 5–6 hardball: 60-minute sessions for girls playing U11 hardball in 2026/27.
  • Intermediate Girls Years 7–8: 60-minute sessions for school and U13 club players – technical, tactical and mental skills.
  • Youth Girls Years 9–10: 90-minute sessions with our pro-coaches, Baradene and Epsom Girls' coaches and guest coaches including former White Ferns.
  • Senior Girls Years 11–13: 90-minute sessions challenging college, U16 and senior club players – tactical, mental and technical.
