Junior Season 2026/27

Years 1–4, Friday-evening junior girls and U10 boys play on Fridays. U11 and up play on Saturdays.

30–31 OctSeason starts
18–19 DecLast games before Christmas
12–13 FebInterclub fixtures restart (internal games from 5 Feb, TBC)
19–20 MarFinal playing day

Note: the U10 boys grade plays most games on Friday evenings this season.

Full junior playing dates
  • Season start: Friday 30 October 2026 – Years 1–4, Friday-evening junior girls and U10 boys. Saturday 31 October – all grades from U11 up, including junior girls.
  • Last day before Christmas: Friday 18 December – Years 1–4, Friday-evening junior girls and U10 boys. Saturday 19 December – all other junior grades from U11 up.
  • Restart after Christmas: Friday 5 February 2027 – internal games only (TBC) for Years 1–4, Friday-evening junior girls and U10 boys. Friday 12 & Saturday 13 February – full interclub fixtures resume.
  • Final playing day: Friday 19 March – Years 1–4, Friday-evening junior girls and U10 boys. Saturday 20 March 2027 – all other junior grades from U11 up.

Scoring

U10 and up are scored live on PlayHQ, Auckland Cricket's online scoring system.

Every player in these grades must have a PlayHQ profile – it's set up once their club registration is complete. Years 2–4 matches are scored manually, not on PlayHQ.

Drug-Free Policy

The safety and wellbeing of all members and the wider community is a priority. Parnell Cricket Club has zero tolerance for illicit drug possession and/or use by playing members, club-affiliated people and members of the community during club activities and/or at club premises. Any breach will be reviewed under the Code of Conduct and may result in disciplinary steps. Questions about this policy go to the Director of Cricket.
