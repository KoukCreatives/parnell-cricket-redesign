Our Story

From a team of teenagers to Auckland's Club of the Year.

On Wednesday 17 March 1858, both of Auckland's daily papers recorded a game between Parnell and Auckland at Albert Barracks. Parnell – a team of teenagers the press called "the Juveniles" – won by an innings and 28 runs, bowling Auckland out for 11.

Parnell players went on to help establish the game around the country, from the first Auckland side in 1860 to New Zealand's first first-class match in 1864. The club affiliated with the Auckland Cricket Association in 1884 and won the championship five times between 1898 and 1914.

Read the full history

Parnell Cricket Club dates back to 1858 and is the oldest club in New Zealand. Both daily papers, The New Zealander and the Southern Cross, recorded a game between Parnell and Auckland on Wednesday 17 March 1858 at Albert Barracks. Parnell won by an innings and 28 runs – our team of teenagers, labelled "the Juveniles" by the press, bowled Auckland out for only 11 in their elders' second innings.

That Parnell side stayed together for several seasons, then set about establishing the game around the country. Auckland challenged and beat Wellington in 1860 – five Parnell members were in that first Auckland side. Two members helped establish Otago cricket and were instrumental in setting up New Zealand's first first-class match in January 1864, Otago v Canterbury (Parnell's John Kissling umpired and GK Turton played), and the first international games – George Parr's All England XI v the XXIIs of Otago and Canterbury.

Back in Auckland, cricket slowly developed. Thanks to Parnell players taking the initiative, the Domain was partly cleared for a field, and overseas teams came again – Lillywhite's All England XI in 1876/77 and the Australians in 1877/78. The Auckland Cricket Association was formed in 1883 and Parnell affiliated in 1884. Over the next decade Australian and English teams visited often, and the Domain was considered one of the most beautiful grounds in the Southern Hemisphere.

Club cricket blossomed in the 1890s with Parnell winning the championship twice, and under the new district scheme the club won it five times between 1898 and 1914. Notable players included Ike Mills, the outstanding Auckland and New Zealand batter, and Cay Olliff, a prolific leg-spinner. Administrators Fred Earl and Bob Lusk were outstanding for the club and in setting up Eden Park and Auckland and New Zealand cricket, and Bill Dinnison became Secretary almost immediately, keeping the club running smoothly for decades.

Between the wars Parnell produced players such as Paul Whitelaw, Merv Wallace and Giff Vivian. Bert Sutcliffe joined straight from Takapuna Grammar in 1940/41 – possibly our greatest batter ever. After WW2, Johnny Hayes spearheaded the attack and toured England with New Zealand in 1949 and 1958.

For years the club sought a permanent home. After the Domain, Eden Park was our base until WW2, then back to the Domain. Hobson Park became available in 1950 and was home until 1966 – a marvellous nursery where Ross Morgan, Bill Playle, Mark Burgess, Terry Jarvis and others grew up under Merv Wallace and Geoff Rabone, with superb administrators such as Peter Badley and Jack Forsman.

Hobson Park was lost to summer hockey, so from 1966 we made do at Eden Park before moving to Orakei Domain in 1984 – which separated our seniors from our juniors until, in 2001/02, Auckland City Council invited us to relocate to the newly reclaimed Shore Road Reserve. A five-bay outdoor net complex and clubroom were quickly built, then the Terry Jarvis Centre, a state-of-the-art practice facility – and, best of all, our junior and senior sections came together under one governing board. That is the club today.

What we strive for

Our Ambition

Over many years we've grown into one of Auckland's most successful clubs – equally passionate about male and female, junior, youth and senior cricket, with administrators driving for success on every front.

Our missionInspiring a love of cricket
Our visionBe the best cricket club in New Zealand

The club has over 750 players, including 600+ juniors, and more than 40 past and current New Zealand representatives – including two current BLACKCAPS and eight current first-class players in our membership. Parnell was Auckland Cricket's Club of the Year in 2019/20, 2020/21 and again in 2023/24 – arguably our most successful senior season ever, winning the Premier Men's T20, the Women's Premier One Day and T20 competitions, and the Premier Reserve Women's T20.

Our values

What We Stand For

Our values are the foundation of our identity and success. They guide our actions, decisions and interactions, and shape the positive, supportive and high-achieving culture that defines Parnell.

Community

Our heartbeat. We work tirelessly to make sure everyone involved with our club is welcome regardless of race, gender, ability or inclination – a place our members come to for friendship, fun and support.

Brave

Brave with the goals we set for our club, the way we lead and how we play on the pitch. We aim for an innovative culture whose initiatives drive agendas New Zealand-wide.

Be Our Best

This underpins the way we play and the spirit in which we meet challenges and opportunities. We will have the best coaches, leaders and staff, set high standards and exceed them.

Passion

For the game and our club. We grow our players and volunteers to be their best and inspire the next generation of cricketers through our enthusiasm.

Respect

Respect runs deep – for those who built our club and for all our members: the way they talk, their views and their opinions. We value them.

Our Ambition

To be the best cricket club in New Zealand – by giving every member a great experience.

Terry Jarvis Centre

A purpose-built indoor sports facility. Parnell Cricket Club initiated and fundraised for it, and set it up for cricket and a range of other indoor sports. Three internationally specified cricket lanes – and nets that retract to create a large open space – make it ideal for aspiring internationals, schools and community groups.

Managed and operated by Parnell Cricket Club on behalf of the Remuera Parnell Sports Community Charitable Trust.

Tucked away on Shore Road with a beautiful outlook over the fields and Ōrākei Basin. The newly furnished clubroom is the perfect spot for a function, with a licensed sun deck for easy indoor–outdoor flow.
