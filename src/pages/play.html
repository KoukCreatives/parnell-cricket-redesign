Years 0–8Junior Boys, Girls & Mixed Grades

This grade introduces the fundamentals of cricket in a fun, engaging and safe environment. With an emphasis on skill development, sportsmanship and team play, it's an excellent foundation for young players to learn and grow.

Each junior team is allocated regular pro-coach led sessions on top of its own team-coach session, so every child gets the right level of training and competition.

Playing opportunities

  • Year 1 – the club's junior programme teaching fundamental cricket.
  • Years 2, 3 & 4 (softball) – 8-a-side teams in the Eastern Districts Cricket Association (EDCA) with Grafton United, Auckland University and Ellerslie.
  • Under 10 – Under 13 (hardball) – for boys and girls in Years 5–8.
  • Junior School Holiday Programme – see coaching.

Representative cricket

  • Hawke's Bay Summer Camps – a very successful tournament in Napier over the Christmas holidays.
  • JT Sparling Super Series – details for the season will be advised.
  • U13 Mixed Summer Series

Winter academy

Boys & Mixed Junior & Youth Academies – see winter academies.

Year 1

Year 1 players join the club's junior programme, built around skill modules and games for a fun, varied session each week. Children this age learn better fundamentals this way than in full games, and later in the season they play limited-over games to test their new skills.

Parents are expected to help when they can – the club runs a pre-season training module for parents. Sessions are on Friday afternoons in front of the clubrooms at Shore Road Reserve (near the playground), 4.45pm to about 6pm, supervised by the club's qualified coaches. Players aren't allocated to teams in Year 1 and there are no mid-week practices. There's a sausage sizzle and the bar is open.

  • Season starts Friday 30 October 2026
  • Last session before Christmas: Friday 18 December 2026
  • Restart: Friday 5 February 2027
  • Final session: Friday 19 March 2027

If weather stops play, we email everyone by 2.45pm. All equipment is provided, so there's no need to buy gear yet.

Years 2, 3 & 4 (softball)

8-a-side teams in the EDCA competition with Grafton United, Auckland University and Ellerslie. In 2026/27 all matches are on Friday evenings: before Christmas, internal games at Shore Road from 5pm; after Christmas some games are at AUCC, Grafton or Ellerslie. Games last about 1.5–2 hours, rules make sure every child gets an equal go, and all three grades use a junior-size Incrediball.

Practices: weekly on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, 4–5pm or 5–6pm, at Shore Road Reserve or Thomas Bloodworth Park – your team coach confirms the time. Pro-coaches also run weekly technical sessions for Years 3 & 4 at the Shore Road outdoor nets.

Team allocations: players should play in their correct grade, though playing up or down is allowed for friends and ability – but once a child is in Year 4 at the start of the season they must play Year 4 (so some may repeat Year 4). Teams mix abilities and schools, and co-ordinators do their best with friend requests, without guarantees.

Equipment: provided in a team gear bag. Contact your Year 2, 3 or 4 grade co-ordinator for more information.

Under 10 – Under 13 (hardball)

For boys and girls in Years 5–8. U11, U12 & U13 teams play Saturday-morning T20 Smash grades around Auckland, split regionally to cut travel; U12 & U13 also play Saturday-afternoon LO30.

  • U10: T20 Smash (hardball) – see the season calendar for this season's day
  • U11: Saturday-morning T20 Smash, 8.30am
  • U12: T20 Smash Saturday 8.30am, plus LO30 Saturday 12.30pm (trials for LO30 only)
  • U13 (half season, pre-Christmas): T20 Smash Saturday 8.30am, plus LO30 Saturday 12.30pm (trials for LO30 only)

Team allocations: U10–U13 players must play in their correct grade by age at 1 April. Exceptionally skilled players may play up one grade with dispensation from the club manager. A player who played up to U12 must repeat U12 – they can't move up to U13, which disbands at Christmas when players switch to college cricket. Playing down needs Auckland Cricket dispensation.

Training: team coaches set weekly times; pro-coaches also run weekly sessions for U10 & U11 at the Shore Road outdoor nets. Each team has a kit bag, though many players have their own gear. Playing conditions are on the Auckland Cricket website.

Years 0–8Junior Girls Only

Whether she's new to cricket or has a few seasons under her belt, our girls' programme is all about a great team experience and the support to be her best – with dedicated Women & Girls pro-coaches, youth coaches, player ambassadors and game-day team coaches.

Team allocations

Club policy is that all players play in their correct grade. Teams are selected to mix abilities and schools as much as possible, and grade co-ordinators do their utmost to accommodate friend requests.

Training

  • Year 0/1–2: skills training is a big part of Friday night cricket – no extra mid-week practices.
  • U9–U13 Girls Only: team trainings on Tuesday afternoons in the Terry Jarvis Centre before Christmas, and at Martyn Wilson Field after Christmas.
  • Pro-coaches lead trainings alongside our Youth Girls coaches and team coaches – a fun environment run by girls, for girls – with parent coaches joining where possible.

Playing opportunities

Girls in Years 0–8 can play in the mixed junior grades alongside boys, or in the Girls Only grades.

  • Year 0/1–2: Friday night Festival of Cricket, 4.45–6pm at Shore Road Reserve in front of the clubrooms (mixed or Girls Only).
  • U9 Friday night: softball skills and small-sided games at Shore Road – ideal for girls new to cricket in Years 3 & 4.
  • U9 Saturday softball: inter-club competition with Parnell, Cornwall and University.
  • U11 softball & U11 hardball: Saturday morning inter-club competitions with Parnell, Cornwall and University.
  • U13 hardball: Saturday morning inter-club competition with Parnell, Cornwall and University.
  • U9 & U11 softball is played at Melville Park, 8–10am (TBC); U11 & U13 hardball is scheduled in the Eastern and Central zones.

Years 9–13Youth Girls

Girls' and women's cricket is a strategic priority of our board, with continued investment across our Youth Girls and Senior Women's programmes.

Grades

  • Under 13 hardball
  • Under 16 hardball – Saturday mornings at Waikaraka Park; girls in their final U16 season are invited to play Premier Reserve T20 on Saturday afternoons
  • Senior Women's grades

Team allocations

Youth girls play within their age group; any dispensation to play up a grade is agreed by the Women & Girls Steering Group. Our pro-coaches identify senior U16 players ready for Senior Women's cricket and give them opportunities through the season, supporting the transition into senior cricket.

Coaches & training

  • Emily Drumm, Director of Women & Girls Cricket & Coaching, oversees the programme and leads the Women & Girls pro-coaching team.
  • U13 & U16 Girls Only trainings: Tuesday afternoons at the Terry Jarvis Centre (indoor nets) or Martyn Wilson Fields (outdoor nets and artificial wicket).
  • Senior Women's trainings are Monday and Thursday evenings; final-year U16 girls playing Senior Women's on Saturdays are invited to the Thursday sessions.

SeniorsSenior Men

For players seeking competitive cricket and strong team camaraderie, with a focus on high-level skill development and match preparation.

Two-day grades

Premier Men, Premier Reserves and Premier Thirds each play in the major competition of their division – Auckland Cricket's competition from October to the end of March, across two-day, one-day and T20 cricket. We may also field a 4XI depending on numbers. The Premier side has won multiple trophies in recent years and the club has won the Simmonds & Osborne Trophy three times in recent seasons.

Training & coaching

Pre-season starts in late August / early September. Prems, Reserves and Thirds train at Shore Road on Tuesday and Thursday evenings, often in the Terry Jarvis Centre indoor nets so we can train in any weather, led by our pro-coaches. All three sides have dedicated team coaches.

Other playing opportunities

  • Presidents (over 35s) – one-day games on Saturday afternoons from early November to the end of March. Practice every Wednesday from 6pm, even in winter.
  • Championship & one-day grades – competitive teams playing from Labour Weekend to the end of March.

Presidents (over 35s)

A keen, enthusiastic side and the backbone of the club – many players have played representative cricket. One-day games on Saturday afternoons from early November to the end of March, and new "older" players are always welcome.

Practice Wednesdays 6–7pm at the Shore Road nets (or the Terry Jarvis Centre if the weather's dubious), with refreshments in the clubrooms after. Contacts: AJ Poyner 021 871 555 · Chris Laycock 021 508 611.

One-day & T20 teams

One-day teams play from Labour Weekend to the end of March. Teams register and pay as a team through the captain or manager (see fees), in full before the season. Training is set by the captain – usually Wednesdays from 6pm at the Shore Road nets.

Players wear the Parnell polo and white shorts from Players Sports, suitable footwear, and a helmet when batting. Results go into PlayHQ by 5pm on the Monday after play. To organise your own team, contact Mark Hooper, Director of Cricket, on 027 246 3848.

The Shore Road nets are reserved for Parnell members: juniors 4–6pm and seniors from 6pm, Monday to Friday.

SeniorsSenior Women

For players aiming to compete at a high level in a supportive team, with an emphasis on continuous skill development and competitive play. All women and girls over 16 should register for Senior Women's cricket.

Grades

Premier is the highest grade of Auckland Cricket's women's competition, followed by Premier Development and Premier Reserve. Every registered player is selected for one of our Saturday teams each week, and all teams are led by pro-coaches at mid-week training and on game days.

Training

  • Pre-season: from September, Monday evenings 6–8pm at the Terry Jarvis Centre.
  • Squad trainings: 6–7.30pm Mondays (indoors at the TJC) and Thursdays (outdoors at Shore Road Reserve).

Representative cricket

ACA U17 Summer Series – a week-long tournament played over three days in January.

Premier, Premier Development & Premier Reserve

Our 1XI plays the ACA Premier Women's competition (T20 and LO40, Saturdays with some Sundays); our 2XI and 3XI play Premier Development and Premier Reserve (T20 and LO35). The Senior Women's coaching team selects all three squads each week, and every registered player is picked for a Saturday team.
