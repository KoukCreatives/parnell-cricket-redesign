Grounds & conditions

Our home is Shore Road Reserve in Remuera, with neighbouring Bloodworth Park and indoor training at the Terry Jarvis Centre. Away grounds include Colin Maiden Park, Cox's Bay Reserve and Orakei Domain.

Playing conditions for every grade are set by Auckland Cricket and published on their website.
