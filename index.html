    Contact us
    How do I register?

    All players register on PlayHQ for the 2026/27 season – this covers both club membership and Auckland Cricket's scoring platform. Subscriptions are paid in full at registration.

    Junior grades are full – what now?

    Email Zak or Steve to join the waitlist. Places do come up during the season.

    Which grade will I play in?

    Juniors and youth are placed by school year (as at 2026). Seniors are graded by ability, from Premier through to social and T20 teams.

    Where are games played?

    Our home is Shore Road, with indoor training at the Terry Jarvis Centre. Away games are listed in the draw on game day.

    What if it rains?

    Check draws & cancellations on the morning of play.

    Can I get a refund?

    Full refund (less a $30 admin fee) until the first day of Term 4; 50% until the first day of play; no refund once the season starts.
