Years 0–8

Juniors

Fun-first cricket with pro-coach sessions. Teams are set by school year, with mixed and girls-only grades.

Year 0–1

A first taste of batting, bowling and catching. No experience needed.

Years 2–4

8-a-side softball cricket in the Eastern Districts competition.

Under 10 – Under 13

Hardball cricket, plus club rep teams: Hawke's Bay Summer Camps, JT Sparling Series, U13 Club Summer Series.

Junior guide for parents

Years 9–13

Youth

A supportive, competitive programme with pathways into rep and senior cricket.

Youth boys

  • U13, U15 and U17 grade cricket – 6 Sundays, limited-overs
  • U15 and U17 Summer Series
  • Eastern Districts U17 and U19 programme
  • Winter academy

Youth girls

  • Under 16 grade – morning, or morning and afternoon
  • Pathways into Senior Women
  • Youth girls transition support
  • Winter academy
Men & women

Seniors

High-performing, fun teams playing two-day, one-day and T20 cricket from October to March.

Senior men

  • Premier, Premier Reserves and Premier Thirds – two-day grades
  • A 4XI when numbers allow
  • Presidents, one-day and T20 teams
  • Premier side: multiple trophies in recent years

Senior women

  • Premier and Premier Reserves
  • 2023/24: three of the four Premier & Reserve titles
  • Coached by Keerthi deSilva, Auckland Coach of the Year
  • University student rates available
