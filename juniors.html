Which grade is my child in?

Teams are set by school year, so your child plays with kids their own age and stage.

Year 0–1

First steps

A fun introduction to batting, bowling and catching through the club's junior programme. No experience needed.

Years 2–4

Softball grades

8-a-side teams with a soft ball, playing in the Eastern Districts Cricket Association against Grafton United, Auckland University and Ellerslie.

U10 – U13

Hardball grades

Full hardball cricket with proper gear. Keen players can also try out for club rep teams – Hawke's Bay Summer Camps, the JT Sparling Series and the U13 Club Summer Series.

Girls only

Girls' grades

Girls can play in mixed teams, girls-only teams, or both. More about girls' cricket

How to sign up

  1. Register on PlayHQ

    Create a PlayHQ account and register your child for Parnell Cricket Club. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Pay the season fee

    Subscriptions are paid in full when you register on PlayHQ. Full refund (less NZ$30) until Term 4 starts.

  3. Meet your team

    Your child's team manager will be in touch before the first game with training times and the draw.

Parent FAQs

My child has never played. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Most of our youngest players are brand new to the game. Year 0–4 cricket is all about learning the basics and having fun.

Registrations are closed – what now?

Email Zak to go on the waitlist. Places do open up during the season and we'll contact you in waitlist order.

Do I need to buy gear?

Not to start. The club has shared gear for hardball grades. Confirm Players' Sports stocks Parnell kit if you'd like your own.

What happens if it rains?

Check the draws & cancellations page on game day. Your team manager will also let you know.

Can parents help out?

Yes please! Every team needs a manager and a coach, and no experience is needed – the club gives you training and resources.

Who do I contact with questions?

Zak van Niekerk, General Manager – zak@parnellcricket.co.nz or 021 0298 9686.
