Become a Sponsor of Parnell Cricket Club

Parnell Cricket Club plays an important role in our community – where we learn life skills, work as a team and often make lifelong friends. As the oldest and one of the fastest-growing clubs in Auckland, we're seeking sponsorship to further improve the experience we offer our members and the wider community.

Four reasons to sponsor a local sports club as a mutually beneficial partnership:

Increase Awareness of Your Company

Your brand in front of parents, coaches, organisers, players and supporters – a positive way to reach your market. Everyone involved with a club likes to support its sponsors.

Make Sport More Affordable for Families

Sponsorship keeps fees low, so more families can afford to play – and more children get the benefits of playing sport.

Build Goodwill in Our Community

Companies that support clubs make a positive contribution locally, and the community gets to understand your company's values.

Possible Tax Incentive

As an added bonus, your business should be able to claim your sponsorship.
